My Reading Year

Upload your Goodreads library export and see how much you read, how fast, how long the books were, and how you rated them.

Your file is read in this browser tab and is never uploaded anywhere.

Drop your Goodreads CSV here Or choose the file from your computer. The file is usually named goodreads_library_export.csv.
How to export from Goodreads
  1. Open goodreads.com and sign in on a computer.
  2. Go to My Books, then Import and export in the left sidebar.
  3. Click Export Library and wait for the file to appear, then download it.
